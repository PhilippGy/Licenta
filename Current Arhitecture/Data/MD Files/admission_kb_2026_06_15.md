# Prezentare generală / Admission rules

## General admission framework at UBB  
- Admission to bachelor's and master's programs is organized by UBB according to the "Regulamentul de admitere la programe de studii de licență și master în Universitatea Babeș-Bolyai" (updated by Senate Decision no. 1/19.01.2026).  
- Admission is by competitive exam or based on academic results and other criteria.  
- Admission is only for accredited or provisionally authorized study programs.  
- Candidates can apply to multiple programs but can be enrolled in at most two programs simultaneously.  
- Students admitted on state-funded places can benefit from funding only for the normal duration of study.  
- International students and EU/EEA/Swiss citizens have the same admission conditions as Romanian citizens, including fees.  
- Special categories of candidates may apply for reserved places (ethnic Romanians abroad, Roma ethnicity, rural high school graduates, persons from social protection system, persons with special educational needs/disabilities, national minorities).  

## Specific to Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science (FMI)  
- Admission is organized by the Faculty Admission Committee under the Dean's authority.  
- Candidates must submit an online application and upload required documents.  
- The admission exam consists of a written test in Mathematics or Computer Science (choice made at registration).  
- The written test is a 3-hour multiple-choice exam with 24 questions.  
- Candidates with high results in the Mate-Info UBB contest (2025 or 2026) or certain national/international olympiads may benefit from admission facilities (e.g., media 10 or partial media increase).  
- Candidates who do not take the written test but apply for programs taught in English or German must upload corresponding language certificates.  
- Candidates with special educational needs or disabilities may request reasonable accommodations during the admission process.  

## Use of Mate-Info UBB Contest results  
- The Mate-Info UBB contest is organized annually as a preparatory contest similar to the admission test.  
- Candidates who score at least 6 in Mate-Info 2026 can use that score as their admission written test score.  
- Candidates who participate in both Mate-Info and the admission test can choose the higher score.  
- Top 10 candidates with scores ≥ 9 in Mate-Info 2025 or 2026 in Mathematics or Informatics receive a media 10 for admission (Category A facility).  
- Other facilities exist for candidates with certain olympiad results (Category B).

# Taxe (fees)

## Admission fees at UBB (general)  
- Admission fee consists of:  
  - Taxa de înscriere (registration fee)  
  - Taxa de procesare (processing fee)  
- Taxa de procesare is charged for organizational and communication aspects, including pre-registration, is non-refundable and non-exemptible.  
- Taxa de înscriere is exempted for:  
  - Employees and children of employees of UBB and the Central University Library  
  - Children of staff (active or retired) in university and pre-university education institutions  
- Taxa de admitere = taxa de înscriere + taxa de procesare.  
- Candidates who are orphans, from single-parent families, from social protection centers, or at risk due to illness, disability, or poverty (up to 26 years old) are exempt from paying the admission fee (both registration and processing).  
- Exemptions require presentation of supporting documents at registration, as per Annex 2 of the UBB Admission Regulation.  
- Fees are paid exclusively online via the Admission Platform (https://admitere.ubbcluj.ro or https://plati.ubbcluj.ro/Event/Details/349 for Mate-Info).  
- Fees are not refunded in case of withdrawal from admission or studies, except for the tuition fee in case of withdrawal before the academic year starts.  

## Mate-Info UBB Contest fee  
- Participation fee: 400 lei (non-refundable, no exemptions).  
- Payment via https://plati.ubbcluj.ro/Event/Details/349.

# Regulament probe / examene (exam regulations)

## Written admission exam at FMI (2026)  
- Exam duration: 3 hours  
- Exam subjects: Mathematics or Computer Science (candidate's choice at registration)  
- Exam format: 24 multiple-choice questions (grilă)  
- Language of exam: language of the candidate's first option; subjects available in Romanian, Hungarian, German, or English  
- Scoring:  
  - Each question has at least one correct and one incorrect answer; no "I don't know" option.  
  - Full points for a question if all and only correct answers are selected.  
  - Partial points if some correct answers are selected without incorrect ones.  
  - Penalty (-0.66 * partial points) if any incorrect answer is selected.  
  - Minimum score per question is 0; if all answers are selected, score is 0.  
- Minimum passing grade: 5.00 (except for Category A candidates who may have media 10)  
- Candidates must bring valid ID (identity card or passport) to exam.  
- Candidates must memorize their contest number received at registration.  
- Entry to exam room is coordinated by room chief, in order of list.  
- Allowed items at desk: water bottle, blue ink pen or permanent writing instrument.  
- Forbidden items: backpacks, bags, mobile phones, smartwatches, or any communication devices (must be left at designated places).  
- Communication, copying, or exchanging materials is forbidden and leads to elimination.  
- Candidates finishing early may leave only after 1 hour 30 minutes from start; those leaving early cannot take exam papers.  
- Candidates cannot enter exam room after exam start and distribution of subjects.  
- Exam papers must be written with blue pen.  
- Candidates receive exam sheets and scratch paper provided by organizers only.  
- Candidates can request new exam sheets if errors are found, but not later than last 30 minutes of exam.  
- Candidates must fill in identification data on exam sheet: contest number (4 digits), full name, father's name, discipline.  
- At exam end, candidates count marked answers and hand in papers under signature.  
- Papers are sealed by supervisors in candidate's presence.  
- Candidates leave exam area after handing in papers.  
- Results are published online according to calendar; papers are corrected in special scanning centers, not in exam rooms.  

## Mate-Info UBB Contest exam regulations (2026)  
- Contest date: 21 March 2026, 09:00  
- Duration: 3 hours  
- Exam format and scoring identical to admission written exam (see above).  
- Candidates must register online and pay participation fee.  
- Contest subjects are in Romanian, Hungarian, German, or English, depending on candidate's choice.  
- Contest results can be used for admission as per facilities described.  
- Contestation of results can be submitted online within 24 hours after results publication.

# Tematici (Exam syllabi)

## Mathematics written test syllabus (Anexa 3A / Anexa 1)  
- Based on current baccalaureate curriculum except financial mathematics (class X).  
- Topics include:  
  - Sets and logic, sequences, functions and graphs, first and second degree functions, vector geometry, trigonometry, real numbers, complex numbers, algebraic structures (groups, rings, fields), matrices, determinants, linear systems, analysis (limits, continuity, derivatives, integrals), combinatorics, geometry, and algebraic equations.  
- Bibliography includes approved school manuals and specialized problem collections for FMI admission.  

## Computer Science written test syllabus (Anexa 3B / Anexa 2)  
- Topics include:  
  - Algorithms, basic programming concepts (Pascal, C, or C++), data types, control structures, subprograms, structured data types, text files, elementary algorithms (divisibility, prime numbers, Fibonacci, sorting, searching), user-defined subprograms, recursion, backtracking, greedy and divide-and-conquer methods, combinatorial generation, graphs (undirected and directed), trees.  
- Bibliography includes approved manuals, problem collections, and online resources (pbinfo.ro).
